Privacy notice

Last updated: 27 July 2026

Deskshift is built to know as little about you as possible. You do not need an account, and your answers are kept on your own device in the first place. We do still process a small amount of data to make the service work. This notice sets out exactly what, why, who it is shared with and what rights you have. We work to the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR.

Who we are

Deskshift is a trading name of MDB Next, a sole trader based in the Netherlands, registered with the Netherlands Chamber of Commerce under number 42063101. MDB Next (“we”, “us”, “Deskshift”) is the controller for the personal data processed through this website. You can reach us at plan@deskshift.pro. Use the same address for privacy questions or to exercise your rights.

The short answer

No account, no profile. We ask for no name, no sign-up, and we do not follow you around with tracking cookies.

On your own device. Your intake answers are stored in your browser's local storage so you can carry on later. We do not keep them centrally on our own servers.

Only for your analysis. To build your insight and your plan, your answers go to an AI service that processes them for that and nothing else. They go nowhere else, and not to your employer.

Sensitive information and your explicit consent

The intake can hold things that feel sensitive: how you feel about your job, what gives you energy or takes it away, and where you are stuck. We treat that as information you would want handled carefully. So we process it only on the basis of your explicit consent, which you give by deliberately starting and working through the intake. You never have to answer a question, and you can stop and wipe everything at any point.

We use these answers solely to build your results. We make no medical, psychological or clinical diagnosis from them, and we do not share them for advertising, profiling or any other purpose.

Our lawful bases

  • Consent (Article 6(1)(a) and, for special category data, Article 9(2)(a)) for processing your intake answers and for sending an email when you ask us to.
  • Performance of a contract (Article 6(1)(b)) for delivering the paid directions and plan once you have paid.
  • Legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)) for limited technical logging, to keep the service secure and working and to prevent abuse.

Who we share data with

We never sell your data. We do use a small number of providers who process data on our behalf, purely to run the service:

  • Anthropic (AI) – processes your intake answers to generate your insight and plan.
  • Brevo (email) – sends the email with your report and the one scheduled reminder a week later, only if you ask for it.
  • Stripe (payments) – takes your payment; card details go straight to Stripe.
  • Vercel (hosting) – hosts the website and handles technical request data.

Transfers outside the UK and the EEA

Some of these providers are based in, or process data in, the United States. That means your intake answers and technical data may be processed outside the UK and the EEA. For those transfers we rely on appropriate safeguards such as the Standard Contractual Clauses, the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, and valid adequacy decisions including the EU–US and UK–US Data Privacy Framework, as applicable to each provider. By starting the intake you consent to that transfer as part of building your results.

How long we keep it

  • Intake answers: stay in your browser's local storage until you clear them yourself, with the delete button or by clearing your browser data. We do not keep them on a central server once your results are built.
  • What goes to the AI service: processed to generate your answer; retention there falls under that provider's policy.
  • Email: the message and the address are processed by our email provider to send and deliver it.
  • Payment data: kept by our payment provider under their policy and statutory retention rules.
  • Server logs: kept only briefly, for security and abuse prevention.
  • Anonymous intake measurement: at most 90 days. A nightly job in our database deletes anything older.

Your rights

You have the right of access, rectification and erasure, the right to restrict and to object to processing, and the right to data portability. Because your intake answers sit on your own device, the fastest route is to delete them yourself with the delete button in the intake or by clearing your browser data.

Where you have given consent, you can withdraw it at any time; that does not affect processing that already took place. For requests about data held by our providers, such as an email that has been sent or a payment, write to plan@deskshift.pro.

You also have the right to complain to a supervisory authority. In the UK that is the Information Commissioner's Office, in Ireland the Data Protection Commission, and in the Netherlands, where we are established, the Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens.

Cookies and storage

We use no tracking cookies and no advertising cookies. To make the intake work we use technical storage in your browser (local storage and session storage), for instance to keep your progress. That storage stays on your own device.

We do measure which step of the intake gets reached, so we can see where people drop out. That runs without a cookie and without a profile: all that is sent is a random session number, which disappears when you close the tab, plus the name of the step. Your answers are not part of it, and there is no way to link that measurement to you as a person. There is no third-party service in between that could follow you across other websites.

Security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your data, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) and the principle of keeping as little as possible centrally. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Children

Deskshift is meant for working adults and is not aimed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Changes

We may update this notice when the service or the law calls for it. The date at the top marks the latest version. Where reasonably possible we will flag significant changes.
